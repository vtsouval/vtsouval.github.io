Vasileios Tsouvalas

I study how machine learning models learn collaboratively, adapt to new tasks, and operate with limited data and compute.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Eindhoven University of Technology. My work spans federated and split learning, efficient foundation-model adaptation and inference, and learning with limited supervision. I also study privacy and fairness in collaborative learning, and the evaluation of language-model reasoning.

Academic profile

Full CV

I completed my PhD at TU Eindhoven in 2025 with Nirvana Meratnia and Tanir Özçelebi. My doctoral research explored how devices can learn collaboratively from heterogeneous data when labels are scarce or noisy and communication resources are limited.

My thesis, Chaos to Harmony, brings together work on data-efficient learning and reducing communication bottlenecks in federated learning. This work underpins my current research on efficient and reliable learning systems.

I have contributed to teaching in Machine Learning for Industry and Internet of Things, and supervised student research in federated and split learning.
