Vasileios Tsouvalas

I am a postdoctoral researcher at TU Eindhoven, building efficient and trustworthy machine learning for edge devices.

I build systems that learn collaboratively from limited or noisy data, adapt multimodal foundation models to edge devices and protect sensitive information. I also investigate efficient inference and how reliably LLMs reason.

I completed my PhD at TU/e in 2025 with Nirvana Meratnia and Tanir Özçelebi, studying collaborative learning with heterogeneous data, scarce or noisy labels, and limited communication. Previously, I earned an MSc in Embedded Systems (TU/e) and a Diploma (Integrated MSc) in Electrical & Computer Engineering (AUTh), both with thesis distinction.
