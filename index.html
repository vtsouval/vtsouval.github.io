Academic profile

Research, talks, conference presentations, and service.

Full CV

I completed my PhD at TU Eindhoven in 2025 with Nirvana Meratnia and Tanir Özçelebi. My doctoral research explored how devices can learn collaboratively from heterogeneous data when labels are scarce or noisy and communication resources are limited.

My thesis, Chaos to Harmony, brings together work on data-efficient learning and reducing communication bottlenecks in federated learning. I now investigate reasoning in language models, alongside my interests in distributed learning and efficient foundation-model adaptation.

I have contributed to teaching in Machine Learning for Industry and Internet of Things, and supervised student research in federated and split learning. I also share this work with the wider research community through talks and open implementations.
